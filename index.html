              Questions

              What is HOPE?

              HOPE, the House of Programming Expertise, is the programming training house of Your College. Selected students train together in batches, with weekly sessions in the lab covering programming foundations, data structures and algorithms, web development, competitive programming and placement preparation.

              How are students selected for a batch?

              The college selects each batch. There is no application on this website: HOPE Elite and HOPE Batch 2 were chosen through the college selection process, and the coordinators announce new batches in class.

              Who teaches the sessions?

              Mentors assigned by the college: faculty from the department, joined by HOPE alumni who come back to run system design and interview sessions. You can meet them in the Mentors section above.

              How does HOPE help with placements?

              Every batch ends with placement preparation: mock interviews with alumni, aptitude practice and resume reviews. Each batch page lists its students' projects and certifications, and the Top placements wall shows where HOPE students have been hired.

              Where can I see each batch's students and projects?

              On the Batches page. Pick HOPE Elite or HOPE Batch 2 to search the students, see the projects they built and the placement board. Alumni have their own page.

              How do I contact the coordinators?

              Email hope@yourcollege.edu. The coordinators reply within a few working days.
